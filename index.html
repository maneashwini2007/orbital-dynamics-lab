Conic Section Lab

CHANGE SPEED TO CHANGE THE ORBIT

For relative separation r and relative velocity v, specific orbital energy determines whether the orbit is bound or unbound.

ε = v²/2 − μ/r   ;   μ = G(m₁ + m₂)
vcircular = √(μ/r)   ;   vescape = √(2μ/r)

ε < 0: ellipse (circle is e = 0) · ε = 0: parabola · ε > 0: hyperbola. For the same initial radius, the preset buttons initialize these regimes.

Physics Primer

THE MODEL UNDER THE HOOD
a₁ = Gm₂ (r₂−r₁) / |r₂−r₁|³
a₂ = −Gm₁ (r₂−r₁) / |r₂−r₁|³
r¨ = −G(m₁+m₂) r / |r|³

Both bodies accelerate toward each other. Their center of mass moves uniformly because internal gravitational forces cancel in pairs (Newton’s third law).

The barycenter is RCM = (m₁r₁ + m₂r₂)/(m₁+m₂).
